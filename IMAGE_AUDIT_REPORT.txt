Date: 23 Sep 2026

Dataset reviewed: 222 places from mumbai_places.csv.
Local image files found: 218.
Image manifest entries found: 10.
Places without a matching local image or manifest entry: 5.

The audit was a visual consistency pass over the supplied image library. It identifies high-confidence mismatches where the image is clearly unrelated to the named destination (for example a bird, anime illustration, flowchart, currency image, unrelated portrait, or another venue). Some generic venue/food photos were marked for review rather than automatically replaced when their relationship to the venue could not be established from the image alone.
